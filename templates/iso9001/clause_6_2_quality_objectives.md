# ISO 9001:2015 - Clause 6.2: Quality Objectives and Planning to Achieve Them

## Purpose
To establish quality objectives at relevant functions and levels, and plan how to achieve them.

## Quality Objectives Framework

{{company_name}} has established quality objectives that are:
- Consistent with the Quality Policy
- Measurable (where practicable)
- Monitored and updated as appropriate
- Communicated to relevant parties
- Available as documented information

## Strategic Quality Objectives

### 1. Customer Satisfaction
**Objective**: Achieve and maintain ≥95% customer satisfaction score  
**Measurement**: Quarterly customer satisfaction surveys  
**Target Date**: Ongoing  
**Resources**: Customer service team, survey tools  
**Responsible**: Sales & Marketing Manager  
**Review Frequency**: Quarterly

**Action Plan:**
- Month 1-3: Establish baseline metrics
- Month 4-6: Implement feedback system
- Month 7-12: Track and improve continuously

### 2. Product/Service Quality
**Objective**: Reduce defect rate to <2%  
**Measurement**: Defects per unit produced/service delivered  
**Target Date**: End of {{generation_date}} year  
**Resources**: Quality control equipment, training  
**Responsible**: Quality Manager  
**Review Frequency**: Monthly

**Action Plan:**
- Implement enhanced inspection procedures
- Train staff on quality techniques
- Analyze defect trends monthly
- Implement corrective actions

### 3. On-Time Delivery
**Objective**: Achieve 98% on-time delivery performance  
**Measurement**: Orders delivered by promised date / Total orders  
**Target Date**: Ongoing  
**Resources**: Production planning system, logistics  
**Responsible**: Operations Manager  
**Review Frequency**: Weekly

**Action Plan:**
- Improve production scheduling
- Enhance supplier coordination
- Monitor delivery performance daily
- Address delays immediately

### 4. Process Efficiency
**Objective**: Reduce production/service cycle time by 15%  
**Measurement**: Average cycle time from order to delivery  
**Target Date**: End of year  
**Resources**: Process improvement team, technology  
**Responsible**: Operations Manager  
**Review Frequency**: Monthly

**Action Plan:**
- Map current processes
- Identify bottlenecks
- Implement lean improvements
- Monitor cycle time reduction

### 5. Employee Competence
**Objective**: 100% of employees trained per competency matrix  
**Measurement**: Training completion rate  
**Target Date**: Ongoing  
**Resources**: Training budget, external trainers  
**Responsible**: HR Manager  
**Review Frequency**: Quarterly

**Action Plan:**
- Develop competency matrices
- Schedule training programs
- Track completion rates
- Verify effectiveness

### 6. Supplier Performance
**Objective**: 95% of suppliers meet quality requirements  
**Measurement**: Supplier quality rating  
**Target Date**: Ongoing  
**Resources**: Supplier evaluation system  
**Responsible**: Procurement Manager  
**Review Frequency**: Quarterly

**Action Plan:**
- Evaluate all active suppliers
- Develop improvement plans for underperforming suppliers
- Recognize top performers
- Review supplier contracts

### 7. Continuous Improvement
**Objective**: Complete 12 improvement projects annually  
**Measurement**: Number of completed projects  
**Target Date**: End of year  
**Resources**: Improvement team, project budget  
**Responsible**: Quality Manager  
**Review Frequency**: Quarterly

**Action Plan:**
- Identify improvement opportunities
- Prioritize projects
- Assign teams and resources
- Track implementation and results

## Departmental Quality Objectives

### Sales Department
- Increase quote accuracy to 98%
- Reduce quote turnaround time to 24 hours
- Achieve 90% customer retention rate

### Operations Department
- Achieve 95% first-pass yield
- Reduce scrap/rework to <3%
- Maintain equipment uptime >95%

### Quality Department
- Complete internal audits per schedule (100%)
- Close corrective actions within 30 days (90%)
- Reduce customer complaints by 20%

### Procurement Department
- Reduce supplier lead times by 10%
- Maintain 99% inventory accuracy
- Qualify 3 new suppliers annually

## Planning to Achieve Quality Objectives

For each objective, we determine:

### What Will Be Done
- Specific actions and activities required
- Process changes or improvements needed
- Resources to be deployed

### What Resources Will Be Required
- **Human Resources**: Staff time, skills, training
- **Infrastructure**: Equipment, facilities, technology
- **Financial**: Budget allocation
- **Information**: Data systems, documentation

### Who Will Be Responsible
- Primary owner assigned for each objective
- Supporting roles and teams identified
- Clear accountability established

### When It Will Be Completed
- Target completion dates set
- Interim milestones defined
- Review points scheduled

### How Results Will Be Evaluated
- Measurement methods defined
- Data collection procedures established
- Success criteria specified
- Review and reporting mechanisms

## Monitoring and Review

### Monthly Reviews
- Process owners review departmental objectives
- Progress tracked against targets
- Issues identified and addressed

### Quarterly Reviews
- Management team reviews all objectives
- Performance trends analyzed
- Resources reallocated as needed
- Objectives adjusted if necessary

### Annual Review
- Comprehensive review during management review
- Achievement assessed
- New objectives set for coming year
- Lessons learned documented

## Communication and Awareness

Quality objectives are communicated through:
- Department meetings
- Performance dashboards
- Individual performance reviews
- Quality bulletins
- Management communications

## Integration with Business Planning

Quality objectives are integrated with:
- Strategic business plan
- Annual budgeting process
- Department operational plans
- Individual performance goals
- Incentive and recognition programs

## Documentation

Quality objectives and plans are maintained in:
- Quality Objectives Register
- Department action plans
- Individual performance agreements
- Management review records

## Updating Quality Objectives

Objectives are updated when:
- Targets are achieved ahead of schedule
- Circumstances change significantly
- New priorities emerge
- Resources change
- Market or customer requirements shift

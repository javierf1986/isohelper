# ISO 9001:2015 - Clause 8.5: Production and Service Provision

## Purpose
To ensure that production and service provision are carried out under controlled conditions to consistently meet customer requirements and deliver conforming products and services.

## 8.5.1 Control of Production and Service Provision

### Controlled Conditions

{{company_name}} implements controlled conditions for production and service provision including:

### 1. Information Availability

**Documentation Available:**
- Product/service specifications
- Work instructions and procedures
- Quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Engineering drawings and models
- Material specifications
- Equipment operating procedures
- Safety and regulatory requirements

**Access:**
- Documents at point of use
- Electronic and hard copy as appropriate
- Current versions only
- Available in appropriate language

### 2. Monitoring and Measuring Resources

**Resources Provided:**
- Measuring equipment (calibrated and verified)
- Test equipment and fixtures
- Inspection tools and gauges
- Monitoring devices
- Process control equipment

**Verification:**
- Regular calibration per schedule
- Verification before use
- Traceability maintained
- Accuracy suitable for purpose

### 3. Monitoring and Measurement Activities

**At Defined Stages:**
- Incoming material inspection
- In-process checks and controls
- First article inspection
- Final inspection and testing
- Statistical process control
- Visual inspections

**Process Monitoring:**
- Critical parameters tracked
- Control limits established
- Out-of-control action defined
- Data recorded and analyzed

### 4. Suitable Infrastructure and Environment

**Infrastructure:**
- Production equipment maintained
- Facilities suitable for purpose
- Utilities and support services
- Information technology systems

**Environment:**
- Temperature and humidity controlled (where critical)
- Cleanliness standards maintained
- Lighting adequate for work
- Noise and ergonomics considered

### 5. Competent Persons

**Personnel Requirements:**
- Qualified and trained operators
- Competence verified and maintained
- Authorized for critical operations
- Supervised as appropriate
- Ongoing training provided

### 6. Validation and Revalidation

**Process Validation:**
- Initial qualification of processes
- Ongoing monitoring and control
- Periodic revalidation
- Validation after changes

### 7. Prevention of Human Error

**Error-Proofing (Poka-Yoke):**
- Design features prevent mistakes
- Visual controls and indicators
- Checklists and verification steps
- Software validation checks
- Automated controls where feasible

### 8. Release, Delivery, and Post-Delivery Activities

**Release Authorization:**
- Acceptance criteria met
- Required inspections completed
- Documentation reviewed and approved
- Authorized personnel release
- Non-conforming product segregated

## 8.5.2 Identification and Traceability

### Product/Service Identification

**Identification Methods:**
- Serial numbers assigned
- Lot/batch numbers
- Date codes
- Part numbers and revisions
- Bar codes and RFID tags
- Electronic tracking systems

**Throughout Realization:**
- Raw materials identified
- Work-in-process tracked
- Finished goods marked
- Status identification (inspected, tested, released)
- Non-conforming product segregated and marked

### Traceability Requirements

**When Traceability is a Requirement:**
- Customer contract specifies
- Regulatory requirement
- Industry standard practice
- {{company_name}} policy

**Traceability System:**

**Forward Traceability:**
- Product to customer/location
- Batch to shipment
- Serial number to end user

**Backward Traceability:**
- Finished product to raw materials
- Product to manufacturing date/lot
- Component to supplier
- Process parameters to product

**Information Recorded:**
- Material lot/batch numbers
- Supplier information
- Manufacturing date and shift
- Equipment used
- Operator identification
- Test results and inspection data
- Customer and delivery information

**Traceability for {{industry}} Industry:**
{% if industry == "Medical Devices" %}
- UDI (Unique Device Identification)
- Device master record linkage
- Manufacturing history record
- Distribution records
- Complaint and recall tracking
{% elif industry == "Aerospace" %}
- Material certifications
- Process certifications
- Non-destructive testing records
- Heat treatment records
- Controlled environmental conditions
{% elif industry == "Food" %}
- Lot coding system
- Ingredient traceability
- Production records
- Distribution tracking
- Allergen control
{% else %}
- Product/batch identification system
- Material traceability to supplier
- Production history records
- Distribution and delivery records
- Quality test results linkage
{% endif %}

## 8.5.3 Property Belonging to Customers or External Providers

### Types of Customer Property

**Physical Property:**
- Customer-supplied materials or components
- Tools, dies, fixtures, and equipment
- Packaging materials
- Returnable containers
- Test equipment
- Samples and prototypes

**Intellectual Property:**
- Designs and specifications
- Proprietary information
- Software and data
- Trade secrets
- Confidential information

### Control of Customer Property

**Receipt:**
- Inspected upon receipt
- Condition documented
- Discrepancies reported to customer
- Accepted or rejected based on criteria

**Identification:**
- Clearly marked as customer property
- Unique identification applied
- Tracked in property log

**Storage:**
- Segregated from {{company_name}} property
- Protected from damage and deterioration
- Secure storage for valuable items
- Environmental controls maintained

**Maintenance:**
- Maintained in good condition
- Preventive maintenance performed
- Calibration maintained (for equipment)
- Damage repaired or reported

**Use:**
- Used only for intended purpose
- Handled by authorized personnel
- Protected during use
- Not used for other customers

**Return:**
- Returned as specified by customer
- Condition documented
- Accompanied by required records
- Acknowledgment obtained

**Loss, Damage, or Unsuitable:**
- Customer notified immediately
- Incident documented
- Investigation conducted
- Corrective action taken
- Replacement or compensation arranged

**Records Maintained:**
- Property log with unique IDs
- Receipt inspection records
- Storage location tracking
- Maintenance and calibration records
- Issue and return documentation
- Incident reports

## 8.5.4 Preservation

### Preservation During Internal Processing

**Identification Preservation:**
- Labels remain legible
- Markings protected from wear
- Electronic identification maintained
- Status indication preserved

**Handling:**
- Appropriate lifting and moving equipment
- Trained personnel handle products
- Damage prevention procedures
- Special handling for fragile items
- ESD protection (where applicable)

**Packaging:**
- Suitable for product type
- Protection from contamination
- Moisture barriers (if needed)
- Cushioning and securing
- Labeling with handling instructions

**Storage:**
- Designated storage areas
- Environmental conditions controlled
- First-in-first-out (FIFO) system
- Shelf-life monitored
- Periodic condition checks
- Segregation of different products

**Protection:**
- Protection from damage and deterioration
- Security from theft or loss
- Protection from contamination
- Climate control where needed
- Pest control measures

### Preservation for Delivery

**Packaging for Shipment:**
- Packaging suitable for transport mode
- Protection from shipping environment
- Compliance with carrier requirements
- Customer-specified packaging used
- Export packaging requirements met

**Documentation:**
- Packing lists included
- Certificates of conformance
- Test reports as required
- Handling and storage instructions
- Material safety data sheets (if applicable)

**Special Requirements:**
- Hazardous material regulations
- Temperature-controlled shipping
- Fragile item precautions
- Security seals applied
- Insurance requirements

## 8.5.6 Control of Changes

### Change Control Process

**Identification of Need:**
- Engineering changes
- Process improvements
- Corrective actions
- Customer requests
- Regulatory changes

**Change Request:**
- Formal change request submitted
- Description of proposed change
- Justification documented
- Urgency level indicated

**Change Review:**
- Technical feasibility assessed
- Impact analysis conducted
- Risk assessment performed
- Resource requirements identified
- Customer notification (if required)

**Approval:**
- Appropriate authority approves
- Multi-disciplinary review
- Customer approval (if required)
- Regulatory approval (if required)

**Implementation:**
- Implementation plan developed
- Training provided as needed
- Documentation updated
- Changes verified effective
- Old materials/processes phased out

**Verification:**
- First article inspection
- Process validation
- Product testing
- Review of results
- Approval to proceed

**Documentation:**
- Change records maintained
- Effectivity dates tracked
- Audit trail preserved
- Lessons learned captured

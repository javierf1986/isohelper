##

 QMS Roles and Responsibilities

### Top Management

**CEO / Managing Director**
- Ultimate responsibility for QMS effectiveness
- Ensures customer focus throughout the organization
- Establishes and communicates Quality Policy
- Ensures QMS resources are available
- Promotes process approach and risk-based thinking
- Ensures QMS achieves intended results
- Leads management review meetings

### Quality Manager

**Responsibilities:**
- Overall responsibility for QMS implementation and maintenance
- Ensures processes deliver intended outputs
- Reports QMS performance to top management
- Promotes customer awareness
- Maintains QMS integrity during changes
- Coordinates internal audits
- Manages document control
- Oversees corrective actions and improvements

**Authority:**
- Stop production for quality issues
- Approve quality procedures and work instructions
- Reject nonconforming products
- Initiate corrective and preventive actions
- Access all areas and records for audits

### Operations Manager

**Responsibilities:**
- Day-to-day production/service delivery operations
- Ensuring products/services meet specifications
- Resource allocation for operations
- Process efficiency and effectiveness
- Staff supervision and work allocation
- Equipment and facility maintenance coordination

**Authority:**
- Approve production schedules
- Allocate operational resources
- Implement process improvements
- Authorize overtime and shift changes

### Sales and Marketing Manager

**Responsibilities:**
- Customer requirement determination
- Contract and order review
- Customer communication
- Customer satisfaction monitoring
- Marketing and business development
- Managing customer complaints

**Authority:**
- Accept or decline orders
- Negotiate contract terms
- Set pricing (within limits)
- Commit delivery dates

### Procurement Manager

**Responsibilities:**
- Supplier selection and evaluation
- Purchase order management
- Supplier performance monitoring
- Incoming material inspection coordination
- Supplier relationship management

**Authority:**
- Approve suppliers
- Issue purchase orders
- Reject nonconforming materials
- Negotiate supplier terms

### Engineering/Design Manager (if applicable)

**Responsibilities:**
- Design and development activities
- Technical specifications
- Design verification and validation
- Engineering change control
- Technical documentation

**Authority:**
- Approve design outputs
- Authorize design changes
- Release products for production

### Quality Control Supervisor

**Responsibilities:**
- Inspection and testing activities
- Calibration program management
- Test equipment maintenance
- Quality data collection and analysis
- Nonconformity identification

**Authority:**
- Accept or reject products
- Hold suspect materials
- Request corrective actions
- Stop processes for quality issues

### Internal Auditor

**Responsibilities:**
- Planning and conducting internal audits
- Audit reporting
- Follow-up on corrective actions
- Maintaining audit records
- Staying current with ISO 9001 requirements

**Authority:**
- Access to all areas and records
- Interview personnel
- Report findings directly to top management
- Request corrective actions

### Document Controller

**Responsibilities:**
- Document creation and revision control
- Distribution of controlled documents
- Maintaining master document list
- Obsolete document removal
- Record retention management

**Authority:**
- Control document distribution
- Maintain document archives
- Implement document changes

### Human Resources Manager

**Responsibilities:**
- Recruitment and selection
- Training program management
- Competence verification
- Performance evaluations
- Training records maintenance

**Authority:**
- Approve training plans
- Authorize training expenditures
- Conduct performance reviews

### All Employees

**Responsibilities:**
- Follow documented procedures
- Report quality issues immediately
- Participate in quality improvement activities
- Maintain competence through training
- Proper use and care of equipment
- Accurate record keeping

**Authority:**
- Stop work for safety or quality concerns
- Suggest improvements
- Report nonconformities

## Communication of Roles

Roles, responsibilities, and authorities are communicated through:

1. **Job Descriptions**
   - Detailed role definitions
   - Updated annually
   - Signed acknowledgment by employees

2. **Organization Charts**
   - Visual representation of structure
   - Posted in common areas
   - Included in Quality Manual

3. **Procedure Documents**
   - Role-specific procedures
   - Responsibility assignments in processes
   - Authority levels defined

4. **Training and Orientation**
   - New employee induction
   - Role-specific training
   - Regular refresher training

5. **Management Meetings**
   - Regular communication forums
   - Decision-making clarity
   - Issue escalation paths

## Delegation of Authority

When authorized personnel are absent, authority is delegated as follows:

- Quality Manager → [Designated backup]
- Operations Manager → [Designated backup]
- Other key roles → [Designated backups]

Delegations are documented and communicated to relevant parties.

## QMS-Specific Authorities

### Customer Focus
**Responsibility**: All managers  
**Authority**: Make decisions that prioritize customer satisfaction

### Risk and Opportunity Management
**Responsibility**: Department managers  
**Authority**: Implement risk treatments within budget

### Continual Improvement
**Responsibility**: All employees  
**Authority**: Propose and implement improvements in their area

### Process Performance
**Responsibility**: Process owners  
**Authority**: Modify processes within scope

## Review and Updates

Roles, responsibilities, and authorities are reviewed:

- Annually during management review
- When organizational changes occur
- Following process changes
- Based on performance issues
- As part of continual improvement

Changes are communicated promptly through appropriate channels.

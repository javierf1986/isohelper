# ISO 9001:2015 - Clause 5.1: Leadership and Commitment

## Purpose
To demonstrate top management's leadership and commitment to the Quality Management System.

## Scope
This document applies to all management activities at {{company_name}}.

## Leadership Responsibilities

### 1. Accountability for QMS Effectiveness
Top management at {{company_name}} demonstrates accountability for the effectiveness of the QMS by:

- Taking responsibility for QMS performance
- Ensuring QMS requirements are established
- Ensuring QMS achieves its intended results
- Directing and supporting staff contribution to QMS effectiveness
- Promoting continuous improvement
- Supporting other management roles

### 2. Customer Focus
Management ensures that:

- Customer requirements are determined and met
- Risks and opportunities affecting conformity are addressed
- Focus on enhancing customer satisfaction is maintained
- Applicable statutory and regulatory requirements are met

### 3. Quality Policy
Top management has established a Quality Policy that:

- Is appropriate to the purpose and context of {{company_name}}
- Provides framework for quality objectives
- Includes commitment to meet requirements
- Includes commitment to continual improvement
- Is communicated and understood within the organization
- Is available to relevant interested parties

### 4. Organizational Roles and Responsibilities
Management ensures that:

- Responsibilities and authorities are assigned and communicated
- QMS conforms to ISO 9001:2015 requirements
- Processes deliver intended outputs
- Performance is reported to top management
- QMS integrity is maintained during changes
- Customer focus is promoted throughout the organization

## Evidence of Leadership
Leadership commitment is demonstrated through:

- Management review meetings (quarterly)
- Resource allocation for QMS
- Active participation in improvement initiatives
- Communication of importance of QMS
- Regular customer interaction

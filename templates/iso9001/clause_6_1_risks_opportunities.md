# ISO 9001:2015 - Clause 6.1: Actions to Address Risks and Opportunities

## Purpose
To ensure the Quality Management System can achieve its intended results, prevent undesired effects, and achieve continual improvement.

## Scope
Applies to all processes and activities within {{company_name}}'s QMS.

## Risk and Opportunity Management Process

### 1. Risk Identification
{{company_name}} systematically identifies risks related to:

**Operational Risks:**
- Production/service delivery failures
- Equipment malfunction
- Supply chain disruptions
- Quality control gaps
- Resource constraints

**Strategic Risks:**
- Market changes in {{industry}}
- Competitive pressures
- Regulatory changes
- Technology obsolescence
- Financial constraints

**Compliance Risks:**
- Non-conformance with ISO 9001
- Regulatory non-compliance
- Customer requirement gaps
- Documentation inadequacies

### 2. Opportunity Identification
Opportunities for improvement include:

- Process optimization
- Technology adoption
- Market expansion
- Customer satisfaction enhancement
- Supplier relationship improvements
- Employee skill development
- Cost reduction initiatives

### 3. Risk Assessment
Each identified risk is evaluated based on:

- **Likelihood**: Probability of occurrence (Low/Medium/High)
- **Impact**: Potential consequences (Low/Medium/High)
- **Risk Level**: Combination of likelihood and impact

**Risk Matrix:**
```
Impact/Likelihood | Low    | Medium | High
High              | Medium | High   | Critical
Medium            | Low    | Medium | High
Low               | Low    | Low    | Medium
```

### 4. Risk Treatment
For each significant risk, {{company_name}} will:

- Develop mitigation actions
- Assign responsibilities
- Set implementation timelines
- Define success metrics
- Allocate necessary resources

### 5. Monitoring and Review
Risk and opportunity management is reviewed:

- Monthly by process owners
- Quarterly during management reviews
- When significant changes occur
- Following incidents or near-misses

## Documentation
All risks and opportunities are documented in the Risk Register, including:

- Risk/opportunity description
- Assessment results
- Treatment actions
- Responsible parties
- Status and effectiveness

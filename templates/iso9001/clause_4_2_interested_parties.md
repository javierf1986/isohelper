# ISO 9001:2015 - Clause 4.2: Understanding the Needs and Expectations of Interested Parties

## Purpose
To identify and monitor interested parties and their requirements relevant to the Quality Management System.

## Scope
This procedure applies to all interested parties of {{company_name}}.

## Interested Parties Identification

### 1. Customer Requirements
{{company_name}} serves customers in the {{industry}} sector with the following key requirements:

- Product/service quality standards
- Delivery timelines
- Technical specifications
- After-sales support
- Compliance certifications

### 2. Regulatory and Legal Requirements
Applicable regulations include:

- ISO 9001:2015 certification requirements
- [Industry-specific regulations - to be defined]
- Local and international standards
- Environmental regulations
- Health and safety requirements

### 3. Supplier and Partner Requirements
Our suppliers and partners expect:

- Clear specifications and requirements
- Timely communication
- Fair payment terms
- Long-term relationships
- Quality feedback

### 4. Employee Requirements
Employee needs and expectations:

- Safe working conditions
- Fair compensation
- Training and development opportunities
- Clear job descriptions
- Recognition and career advancement

### 5. Shareholders/Owners
Ownership expectations:

- Financial performance
- Business growth
- Risk management
- Compliance and reputation
- Sustainability

## Monitoring and Review
Interested parties and their requirements are reviewed:

- Quarterly through stakeholder feedback
- During management review meetings
- When changes in context occur
- Through customer satisfaction surveys

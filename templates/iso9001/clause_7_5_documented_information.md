# ISO 9001:2015 - Clause 7.5: Documented Information

## Purpose
To control documented information required by the QMS and by ISO 9001:2015, ensuring it is available, suitable for use, and adequately protected.

## Documented Information Requirements

{{company_name}} maintains documented information as required by:
- ISO 9001:2015 standard
- Our Quality Management System
- Applicable statutory and regulatory requirements
- Customer requirements

## Types of Documented Information

### 1. QMS Documentation
- **Quality Manual**: Overall QMS description and scope
- **Quality Policy**: Statement of quality commitment
- **Quality Objectives**: Measurable targets
- **Organizational Structure**: Roles and responsibilities

### 2. Procedures and Work Instructions
- **Procedures**: How key processes are performed
- **Work Instructions**: Detailed step-by-step guidance
- **Forms and Templates**: Standardized documents
- **Specifications**: Technical requirements

### 3. Records
- **Quality Records**: Evidence of conformity
- **Performance Records**: Process and product data
- **Training Records**: Competence evidence
- **Calibration Records**: Equipment verification
- **Audit Records**: QMS conformity evidence

## 7.5.2 Creating and Updating

### Document Creation

**Creation Process:**
1. Identified need for new document
2. Draft prepared by competent personnel
3. Technical review by subject matter experts
4. Approval by authorized personnel
5. Assignment of document number and version
6. Entry into document control system
7. Distribution to relevant users

**Document Format:**
- Appropriate title and description
- Unique identification (document number)
- Version/revision number and date
- Author and approver identification
- Classification (if applicable)
- Distribution list
- Review/revision history

### Document Review and Update

**Review Frequency:**
- Procedures: Annually or as needed
- Work Instructions: Annually or when process changes
- Forms: Annually or as needed
- Records: Retention per schedule

**Update Process:**
1. Change request initiated
2. Impact assessment conducted
3. Revisions drafted
4. Review and approval obtained
5. New version issued
6. Obsolete version withdrawn
7. Changes communicated to users

**Change Control:**
- Reasons for changes documented
- Changes clearly identified
- Version history maintained
- Re-approval after updates

## 7.5.3 Control of Documented Information

### 7.5.3.1 Availability and Suitability

**Availability:**
- Documents available at point of use
- Accessible through document management system
- Electronic and/or hard copy as appropriate
- Available to relevant external parties when needed

**Suitability:**
- Documents legible and identifiable
- Appropriate format (language, version, graphics)
- Suitable for intended use
- Properly reviewed and approved before use

**Distribution Control:**
- Master document list maintained
- Controlled copies issued
- Distribution records kept
- Users notified of updates
- Obsolete documents removed from use

### 7.5.3.2 Protection and Preservation

**Protection Measures:**

**Confidentiality:**
- Access control based on need-to-know
- Confidentiality markings applied
- Secure storage for sensitive documents
- Non-disclosure agreements where appropriate

**Integrity:**
- Protection from unauthorized changes
- Version control enforced
- Backup systems for electronic documents
- Regular backup verification

**Availability:**
- Disaster recovery procedures
- Off-site backups maintained
- Redundant storage systems
- Cloud storage (where appropriate)

**Physical Protection:**
- Controlled environmental conditions
- Protection from damage, deterioration, loss
- Fire protection systems
- Secure storage facilities

### Access and Retrieval

**Access Control:**
- Authorization levels defined
- User permissions managed
- Audit trail of access maintained
- External party access controlled

**Retrieval:**
- Logical filing and indexing system
- Quick search and retrieval capability
- Version identification clear
- Archived documents accessible when needed

## Document Control System

### Master Document List

**Information Maintained:**
- Document number and title
- Current version and date
- Author and approver
- Distribution locations
- Review frequency
- Status (active/obsolete)

### Document Identification

**Numbering System:**
```
Format: [Type]-[Department]-[Sequence]-[Version]
Example: PROC-QA-001-Rev02
```

**Document Types:**
- PROC: Procedures
- WI: Work Instructions
- FORM: Forms and templates
- SPEC: Specifications
- REC: Records

### Version Control

**Version Numbering:**
- Initial release: Version 1.0 or Rev 00
- Minor changes: Increment decimal (1.1, 1.2)
- Major changes: Increment whole number (2.0, 3.0)

**Change Identification:**
- Revision bars or highlighting for changes
- Summary of changes documented
- Date of revision recorded

**External Document Management:**

## Records Management

### Record Requirements

**Records Generated:**
- Inspection and test records
- Training records
- Audit reports
- Management review minutes
- Corrective action reports
- Customer complaint records
- Calibration records
- Supplier evaluation records

### Record Control

**Identification:**
- Unique identification for each record
- Linkage to relevant process/product
- Date and responsible person recorded

**Storage:**
- Protected from damage and loss
- Appropriate environmental conditions
- Secure and accessible locations
- Electronic backup systems

**Retrieval:**
- Easy and timely retrieval
- Organized filing system
- Indexed for quick location
- Access restricted as needed

**Retention:**
| Record Type | Retention Period | Disposal Method |
|-------------|------------------|-----------------|
| Quality Records | {{industry}} standard / 5 years minimum | Shred/Delete |
| Training Records | Duration of employment + 3 years | Shred/Delete |
| Audit Records | 3 years | Shred/Delete |
| Calibration Records | Life of equipment + 1 year | Shred/Delete |
| Customer Records | Per contract / 7 years | Shred/Delete |

**Disposition:**
- Disposed per retention schedule
- Secure destruction methods
- Disposition records maintained
- Legal/regulatory holds observed

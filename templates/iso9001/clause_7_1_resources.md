# ISO 9001:2015 - Clause 7.1: Resources

## Purpose
To determine and provide resources needed for the establishment, implementation, maintenance, and continual improvement of the Quality Management System.

## Resource Management at {{company_name}}

{{company_name}} recognizes that adequate resources are essential for QMS effectiveness and achieving quality objectives. We systematically identify, provide, and maintain resources needed for our operations.

2. **Infrastructure** - Buildings, equipment, IT systems
3. **Environment** - Physical, social, psychological conditions
4. **Monitoring and Measuring Resources** - Equipment and tools
5. **Organizational Knowledge** - Knowledge base and information

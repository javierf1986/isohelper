# ISO 9001:2015 - Clause 4.4: Quality Management System and Its Processes

## Purpose
To establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve the Quality Management System, including the processes needed and their interactions.

## Process Approach

{{company_name}} has adopted a process approach to our Quality Management System. This ensures that processes are:

- Understood and consistently applied
- Managed as an interrelated system
- Optimized for effectiveness and efficiency

## QMS Process Network

### Core Business Processes

#### 1. Management Processes
**Purpose**: Provide direction and ensure QMS effectiveness

- **Strategic Planning**
  - Input: Market analysis, stakeholder needs, performance data
  - Output: Strategic objectives, resource allocation plans
  - Owner: Top Management
  
- **Management Review**
  - Input: QMS performance data, audit results, customer feedback
  - Output: Decisions on improvement, resource needs
  - Owner: Quality Manager
  - Frequency: Quarterly

- **Risk and Opportunity Management**
  - Input: Context analysis, process performance
  - Output: Risk treatment plans, improvement opportunities
  - Owner: Risk Manager

#### 2. Operational Processes
**Purpose**: Deliver products/services to customers

- **Sales and Order Processing**
  - Input: Customer inquiries, market demands
  - Activities: Quote preparation, order review, contract management
  - Output: Confirmed orders, customer requirements
  - Owner: Sales Manager
  - KPIs: Order accuracy, response time

- **Design and Development** (if applicable)
  - Input: Customer specifications, regulatory requirements
  - Activities: Design planning, development, verification, validation
  - Output: Design outputs, specifications
  - Owner: Engineering Manager
  - KPIs: Design cycle time, change requests

- **Procurement**
  - Input: Material requirements, approved supplier list
  - Activities: Supplier evaluation, purchase order management, receiving inspection
  - Output: Qualified materials, supplier performance data
  - Owner: Procurement Manager
  - KPIs: Supplier quality rating, on-time delivery

- **Production/Service Delivery**
  - Input: Customer orders, specifications, materials
  - Activities: {{industry}}-specific production/service activities
  - Output: Finished products/delivered services
  - Owner: Operations Manager
  - KPIs: Production efficiency, first-pass yield, on-time delivery

- **Inspection and Testing**
  - Input: Products/services, specifications
  - Activities: In-process inspection, final inspection, testing
  - Output: Quality records, conformity status
  - Owner: Quality Control Supervisor
  - KPIs: Defect rate, inspection effectiveness

- **Customer Service**
  - Input: Customer feedback, warranty claims, complaints
  - Activities: Issue resolution, technical support, satisfaction monitoring
  - Output: Resolved issues, customer satisfaction data
  - Owner: Customer Service Manager
  - KPIs: Resolution time, customer satisfaction score

#### 3. Support Processes
**Purpose**: Provide necessary resources and support

- **Human Resources Management**
  - Activities: Recruitment, training, competence verification
  - Output: Competent personnel, training records
  - Owner: HR Manager

- **Infrastructure and Maintenance**
  - Activities: Equipment maintenance, calibration, facility management
  - Output: Operational equipment, maintenance records
  - Owner: Maintenance Manager

- **Document and Record Control**
  - Activities: Document approval, distribution, revision control
  - Output: Current documents, controlled records
  - Owner: Document Controller

- **Internal Audit**
  - Activities: Audit planning, execution, reporting
  - Output: Audit reports, nonconformities
  - Owner: Internal Auditor
  - Frequency: Annual cycle

#### 4. Improvement Processes
**Purpose**: Ensure continual improvement

- **Nonconformity Management**
  - Input: Defects, customer complaints, audit findings
  - Activities: Investigation, root cause analysis, corrective action
  - Output: Corrective actions, effectiveness verification
  - Owner: Quality Manager

- **Continual Improvement**
  - Activities: Process analysis, improvement projects, innovation
  - Output: Process improvements, cost reductions
  - Owner: Improvement Team

## Process Interactions and Sequence

### Process Flow Map
```
[Customer Requirements] 
        ↓
[Sales & Order Processing] → [Contract Review]
        ↓
[Design & Development] (if applicable)
        ↓
[Procurement] ← → [Supplier Management]
        ↓
[Production/Service Delivery] → [Inspection & Testing]
        ↓
[Packaging & Delivery]
        ↓
[Customer Service & Feedback]
        ↓
[Performance Evaluation] → [Management Review] → [Improvement Actions]
```

### Key Interfaces
- **Sales ↔ Production**: Order requirements and capacity planning
- **Procurement ↔ Quality**: Supplier quality and incoming inspection
- **Production ↔ Maintenance**: Equipment availability and performance
- **All Processes ↔ Document Control**: Procedure availability and currency

## Process Management Requirements

### For Each Process, We Determine:

1. **Inputs Required**
   - Information, materials, resources needed

2. **Activities and Methods**
   - Sequence of operations
   - Work instructions and procedures

3. **Outputs Expected**
   - Products, services, information delivered

4. **Resources Needed**
   - Personnel, equipment, infrastructure

5. **Responsibilities**
   - Process owners and key personnel

6. **Risks and Opportunities**
   - Potential issues and improvement areas

7. **Performance Criteria**
   - KPIs and measurement methods

8. **Monitoring and Measurement**
   - How process performance is tracked

9. **Improvement Actions**
   - How the process is improved

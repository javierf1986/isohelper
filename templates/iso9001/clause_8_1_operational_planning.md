To ensure processes needed to meet requirements for products and services are planned, implemented, and controlled.

- Change request documentation

For outsourced processes, {{company_name}} ensures:

- Performance is monitored
- Records are maintained

- On-time delivery
- Process efficiency

- Weekly at operational level
- Monthly by department managers
- Quarterly during management review
